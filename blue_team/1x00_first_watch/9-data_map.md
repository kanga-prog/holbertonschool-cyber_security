# Data Map and Protection Gap Analysis

## Purpose

This document maps the major sensitive data categories handled by MedDefense Health Systems across the three data lifecycle states:

- **At Rest**: where the data is stored.
- **In Transit**: how the data moves between systems, users, and sites.
- **In Use**: who uses the data and on which systems or devices.

The objective is to identify whether each data category is protected appropriately for its classification level and to document specific protection gaps.

## Data Classification Reference

| Classification | Definition |
|---|---|
| Restricted | Highest sensitivity. Unauthorized access causes severe harm, regulatory exposure, patient safety risk, or major financial/legal impact. |
| Confidential | Sensitive internal information. Unauthorized access causes significant harm to the organization, employees, partners, or operations. |
| Internal | Internal operational information not intended for public disclosure, but with limited impact if exposed. |
| Public | Information intended for public consumption. |

## Data Risk Summary

MedDefense's most significant data protection weakness is the exposure of **Restricted clinical and patient data in transit and in use across a flat internal network with weak access controls**. Patient records, lab results, imaging studies, medication data, billing data, and medical device data all depend on systems that are reachable across internal subnets without effective segmentation. This creates a wide gap between the sensitivity of the data and the level of protection applied: a compromised workstation, unmanaged device, shared credential, or exposed service could provide a path to Restricted data, while weak session handling at nurse stations and broken patient portal authorization show that data-in-use and application-level controls are also insufficient.
